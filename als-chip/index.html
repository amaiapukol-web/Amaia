What would count as evidence?

Compare baseline culture conditions with added IGF-1, IGF-2 and both. Measure cell survival, axon integrity, neuromuscular junctions, muscle force and pathway markers. Verify exposure concentrations and distinguish direct muscle effects from motor-neuron effects. The display shows no experimental outcomes.
