What the controls mean

The IGF-2 slider changes a proposed input concentration only. It does not predict how much IGF-2 reaches cells, Akt activation, motor-neuron survival, or muscle force. Those are precisely the outcomes that would need to be measured experimentally. IGF-1 in the original muscle medium must be accounted for when designing controls.
